Hop On! privacy policy

Effective 23 October 2026

In short: no ads, trackers or analytics, and nothing to buy. Your progress stays on your phone. The game does not send anything to us or to anyone else.

Who we are

Hop On! is an Android game made by attnom. If you have a question about this policy, write to admin@attnom.com.

What the game stores

To remember your game, Hop On! keeps your levels, stars, scores, coins, town, unlocked bus colors and settings in the app's own storage on your phone. It keeps a second copy on the same phone, so a damaged save can be restored. This data never leaves your phone through the game, and we never receive it.

The game does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location, photos or any other personal information. It has no account to create.

What the game does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising ID.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools.
  • No in-app purchases. Coins are earned by playing and can't be bought.
  • No chat and no way to share anything with other players.

Android lists internet access for the app because the framework the game is built with includes it. This version of Hop On! makes no internet connections.

Your phone's own backup

If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include the game's data in your phone's backup to your Google account, as it does for other apps. That backup is run by Google under the Google Privacy Policy, and we can't see it. You can turn it off in your phone's settings.

Google Play Games (if added later)

This version does not use Google Play Games. A later version may let you sign in to it. Signing in will be optional, and the whole game will still work without it. If you sign in:

  • your Play Games name and scores appear on the game's leaderboards, where other players can see them;
  • your achievements are recorded in your Play Games profile;
  • your progress is saved to your own Google cloud save, so it can follow you to another phone.

Google runs these services under the Google Privacy Policy. We don't receive your Google account details. We will update this page before that version is released.

Children

Hop On! does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. It has no ads, no purchases and no way to contact other players.

Deleting your data

  • Uninstall the game. This deletes everything it stored on your phone.
  • Or open Settings in the game and tap Reset progress. This deletes your levels, stars, coins and town.

If you use Google Play Games in a later version, Reset progress can also reset your cloud save; the game says which before anything is deleted. You can delete your Play Games profile and its data in the Play Games app or your Google account settings.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever starts to handle data differently, this page will say so before the update that does it, and the date at the top will change.
